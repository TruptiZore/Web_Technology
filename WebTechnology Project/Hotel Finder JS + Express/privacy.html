Privacy Policy

At Hotel Finder, your privacy is our top priority. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Any information collected—such as your name or booking dates—is stored securely entirely on your local device (via LocalStorage) for an optimal, lightning-fast experience without relying on external databases.

By using Hotel Finder, you agree to a fast, private, and premium booking experience.
